Privacy Policy

Remi Receipt Bot · Last updated 18 November 2026

Remi is a WhatsApp bot that helps businesses create receipts and invoices. This page explains what information Remi collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, so Remi can reply to you and keep your account.
  • Business details you send: business name, address, contact number, bank details shown on invoices, your logo, and your chosen receipt layout and colours.
  • Receipt and invoice details: customer names, items, amounts and dates you send so Remi can build each document, plus a simple sales record used for your sales summaries.
  • Team details, if you invite staff with an invite code: which phone numbers belong to your business.
  • Subscription details, if you upgrade: your plan, its dates, and the email you give for checkout. Card payments are handled by our payment provider; Remi never sees or stores card numbers.

How we use it

  • To create and send your receipts and invoices on WhatsApp.
  • To remember your business settings so you don't have to repeat them.
  • To show you your own sales summaries.
  • To manage your subscription.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Services that process your data

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) delivers messages and documents.
  • Google Firebase stores your account and business details.
  • Google Gemini reads the messages and photos you send to turn them into receipt details. Only what you send for a receipt is shared with it.
  • Render hosts the server that runs Remi.
  • Our payment provider processes subscription payments.

How long we keep it

We keep your business details and sales records while your account is active. Documents sent through WhatsApp are kept by WhatsApp for up to 30 days for delivery. You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all related data, email remireceiptbot@gmail.com from any address, including the WhatsApp number you used with Remi. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email or WhatsApp.

Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase and accessed only by Remi's server using private credentials. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to what Remi needs to work.

Children

Remi is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
